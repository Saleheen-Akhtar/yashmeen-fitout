# Image Prompts

## Master prompt

Warm editorial architectural photography for a Dubai fit-out studio: considered interiors, natural material texture, timber joinery, stone, brass details, controlled daylight, quiet luxury, no generic staged office, no text overlays, no logos, realistic construction detail, restrained warm neutrals.

## Section prompts

- Hero: finished waterfront villa interior with timber joinery and warm daylight.
- Workshop: close editorial view of bespoke joinery being made in an Al Quoz workshop.
- Portfolio: a sequence of distinct Dubai villa, office and hospitality interiors, photographed as completed architecture.
- Process: site and workshop coordination, material samples, drawings and measured installation detail.

## QA

Check that images support the claim being made, have no invented signage, do not imply a project is completed without approval, and remain legible with the light palette.
